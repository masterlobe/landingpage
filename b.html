Questions

Before you decide.

Everything important, upfront.

Is ₹3,000 the complete cost? +

₹3,000 is the development cost for the starter website. Domain and hosting are separate and depend on your preferences. They can range from free options to around ₹3,000 per year.

What exactly do I get for ₹3,000? +

You get a professionally designed, mobile-responsive website personalised for your clinic, including your clinic information, doctors, services, contact options and location, plus setup and deployment.

What happens if I want more later? +

You can upgrade whenever you need more pages, features or functionality. If you choose to upgrade to a larger website, your ₹3,000 starter payment can be credited toward the upgrade.

What if I don't want to upgrade? +

That's completely fine. The ₹3,000 website is a complete starter website on its own. Upgrading is optional.

Do you guarantee patients or enquiries? +

No. We don't believe a website can honestly guarantee patients, enquiries or revenue. The point of starting small is to give you a professional online presence without making a large upfront bet.

Do I need technical knowledge? +

No. We handle the technical setup and deployment. You provide your clinic information, logo, images and other details we need to personalise the website.

How long does it take? +

The starter website is typically ready within 3 days once we have the required clinic information and assets.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting? +

Not necessarily. We can discuss the options based on what you prefer. There are free hosting options, while paid domain and hosting choices can cost up to around ₹3,000 per year depending on the setup you choose.
